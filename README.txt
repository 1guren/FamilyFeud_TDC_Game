FAMILY FEUD - LIVE OPERATOR GUIDE

Run the game:
1. Open index.html in a modern browser (Chrome/Edge recommended).
2. Enter team names and press START GAME.
3. At the start of every question, select the team that buzzed first.

Controls:
- Click a team scorecard during FACE-OFF = that team becomes Team A / controlling team
- Click an answer card, or press 1-6 = reveal a correct answer
- X = wrong answer / strike (unlimited presses; every press replays the X animation and buzzer)
- A = Team 1 buzz during face-off
- L = Team 2 buzz during face-off
- V = Reveal Remaining Answers after a question ends (no points added)
- N = Next Question / Next Round / Final Result
- U = Undo the last gameplay action
- H = Show/hide the small host control panel
- F = Fullscreen
- R = Return to intro from the final result screen

FINAL QUESTION BANK SCORING:
- Correct Team A answers add their printed value to the temporary Question Bank.
- Team totals do NOT increase while a question is still active.
- Team A has up to 3 strikes.
- If Team A reveals every answer before the 3rd strike, Team A wins the entire Question Bank.
- After Team A's 3rd strike, Team B gets EXACTLY ONE steal answer.
- If Team B's steal answer is CORRECT, that answer's value is added to the Question Bank and Team B wins the ENTIRE bank immediately.
- If Team B's steal answer is WRONG, Team A wins the existing Question Bank immediately.
- There is NO steal-back and NO second steal cycle.
- When the question ends with hidden answers, Reveal Remaining Answers shows them for display only and gives 0 additional points.
- The Question Bank resets to 0 at the start of every new question.
- Permanent team totals carry across all 9 questions and all 3 rounds.

Game structure:
- 3 rounds only.
- Exactly 3 questions per round.
- Normal scoring only: no double points, triple points, or multipliers.
- Round 3 Question 3 is the final question.
- Highest accumulated team total wins; equal totals show a Tie result.

On-screen status:
- FACE-OFF: waiting for the first team to buzz.
- NORMAL: shows the controlling team and strikes used/remaining out of 3.
- STEAL: shows Team B and that it has exactly one answer to win the entire Question Bank.
- QUESTION ENDED: shows which team won the bank and whether hidden answers remain.

Live-event safety:
- The X button never disables. Every click produces a fresh X animation and buzzer sound.
- Logical strike counts are capped by the current phase.
- Rapid extra X clicks immediately after the 3rd strike are guarded so they cannot accidentally count as Team B's steal miss.
- Undo can restore the previous gameplay state if the operator makes a mistake.
